Our design process went through a focused narrowing-down journey. We started from a broad topic: the social challenges faced by international students on campus (Folder 1). Then we gradually narrowed down to a specific context with high relevance — the Quiet Zone in libraries (Folder 2), where the peaceful atmosphere often makes it difficult for students to ask for help, even when they need it.
Building on this, we developed clear user personas (Folder 3) and conducted multiple rounds of interviews and needs analysis to better understand the pain points. Eventually, we shaped the idea of Nudgee-Q and improved our prototype through user surveys and small-scale evaluations (Folder 4).
